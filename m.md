Whenever someone asks me,

“Do you know a good software engineer we can hire?”

I never open LinkedIn first.

I start by reaching out to engineers I’ve actually worked with.

Why?

Because I can confidently put my name behind them.

If I don’t find someone there, I reach out to engineers in my community.

Still looking? I ask people I trust to recommend engineers they’ve worked with.

Notice the pattern?

Recommendations don’t start with resumes.

They start with reputation.

“𝐃𝐨 𝐲𝐨𝐮 𝐤𝐧𝐨𝐰 𝐚𝐧𝐲𝐨𝐧𝐞?”

And when that question comes, people don’t remember who had the fanciest LinkedIn banner.

They remember who consistently delivered.

Who made their lives easier.

Who was reliable.

Who was someone they’d gladly work with again.

You see why should treat every opportunity like someone is deciding whether they’ll recommend you in the future, because they probably are.
